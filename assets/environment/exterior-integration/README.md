# v180 landscape integration assets

This pass keeps the existing sourced mountains and vegetation. It introduces continuous terrain grading and source-derived material copies, not new mountain models or a replacement exterior pack. Original provenance remains in [exterior-composition](../exterior-composition/README.md) and [lab-art](../lab-art/README.md). Existing unresolved Foggy/constituent rights remain unchanged; those assets are not newly used here.

Preparation used the installed **Blender 5.2.3 LTS**, build `e13b728c6880`, at `.tools/blender-5.2.3-windows-x64/blender.exe`. Original sources and the prior native adaptation were preserved. `prepare_integration.py` works from the private copy, exports new grading and softer color maps, and generates `layout.json` / `src/ExteriorIntegrationLayout.luau`.

- **Mountain geometry:** existing AlpineRange mesh `131140296893821`, unchanged 13,999 triangles. Five instances retain the v180 layout's valley opening. `composition.json` records each selected distance/height/rotation adjustment. The northwestern group remains stronger, western/southern ranges are lower and farther, and the valley's far range moves from radius 3,520 to 4,600.
- **Rock material:** supplied color maps are downsampled to 256 then exported at 512 pixels, with 9% desaturation and 17% compression toward a muted natural tint. UVs and geometry remain unchanged; no normal microdetail is added. Active maps: Alpine `85297558896142`, buttress `95023241778608`. The prepared cliff map `138974423848991` is unused after retiring the exposed open-backed scan sheets. Original maps remain preserved.
- **Ground:** 64 adjoining 1,500-stud meshes, 512 triangles each (32,768 total; v180 used 8,192). Smooth grading adds low foreground variation, asymmetric foothills and a shallow, irregular valley. Ground within the dome footprint remains at Y −8; no elevation of the whole exterior. Shared analytical normals match across tile borders. A single continuous 1K color map (`135605946295465`) varies meadow/woodland/rocky soil tones without material seams or periodic stripes. No road, gate, playable terrain or scenery billboards.
- **Planting:** all 102 v180 trees and 88 meadow objects retain their X/Z positions, yaw, size and color; Y is reseated on the revised ground or mountain surface. 94 trees and 104 low plants/rocks extend the existing groups toward foothills and along irregular valley banks. Original broadleaf, conifer, shrub and fern assets remain. Exact placements are deterministic and stored in `layout.json`.
- **Removed placements:** seven open-backed `StrataCliff` scan sheets from `RockyFoothills` produced exposed thin fragments under the new grading. Their generating calls were removed from `ExteriorCompositionBuilder`; the native template/source is retained. Seven `RockButtress` instances remain, seated using nearby ground minima. The v180 flat grading is replaced; its templates remain private/native recovery assets. No other legacy assembly was found or removed in this focused pass.

The three active newly uploaded maps returned creator **User 291892217**. Actual ground mesh IDs and final texture IDs are recorded in `imported-assets.json`. Imported intermediate flat-normal tiles and fallback material IDs are historical private records, not active placements. All scene geometry is anchored and noncolliding/nonquerying/nontouching.

For library recovery, normalize each imported ground template to the `layout.json` ground `size` and set its `SourceCenter` to the recorded world-space `center`. X mirroring is already handled by the preparation script. Library texture attributes are recorded in `imported-assets.json`; the active recipe applies them to clones. Native recovery files already contain the normalized library. Estimated instantiated source triangles are **1,226,210** (v180: 750,035); the richer planting increases geometry despite reduced texture detail.
