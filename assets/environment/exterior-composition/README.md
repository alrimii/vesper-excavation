The three model geometries total **343,806 → 27,998 unique triangles** (91.9% reduction in prepared source geometry). This is not a measured frame-rate improvement. Blender **5.2.3 LTS**, executable `.tools/blender-5.2.3-windows-x64/blender.exe`, build `e13b728c6880`, was actually used. No additional Blender installation was needed. Blender emitted a shutdown/cache warning after writing the verified blend/FBX outputs.

All three mesh IDs and the corrected terrain texture returned creator **User 291892217 (Alrimi)** from Roblox asset metadata. `imported-assets.json` records every actual mesh/texture ID, including the 64 low-ground tiles. The native FBX importer selected the range's bedrock texture as its merged material; the supplied terrain color map was uploaded separately and explicitly assigned. The unused imported fallback ID remains recorded, not used by the scene.

`prepare_assets.py` imports the original files, decimates them, keeps UVs, removes fine normal detail and exports the private native master/FBX. Its only original geometry is a continuous low ground apron, **not mountain geometry**: 64 adjoining 1,500-stud tiles, 128 triangles each. Native X mirroring is accounted for during library setup. Templates are normalized to `geometry.json` sizes and have `SourceCenter = (-center.x, center.y, center.z)`. The live recipe applies vertical relief scale 2.7 and Y offset 16.3 to the apron; central ground stays below the laboratory floor. No terrain editing or new outdoor collision world is involved.

Native library: `ServerStorage.ExteriorCompositionAssets` (67 templates). Recipe: `ServerStorage.VesperTools.ExteriorCompositionBuilder`, canonical `src/ExteriorCompositionBuilder.luau`. Ground has no image texture, using Roblox Grass material and one coherent color. The distant landscape uses actual opaque geometry; there are no scenery billboards or alpha-cutout backdrop panels. Two very faint ground-level mist beams use Roblox's built-in smoke texture.

Retained trees/plants come from the already imported [Quaternius / KayKit library](../lab-art/README.md). No additional vegetation pack was downloaded. Older Foggy/Blendkit libraries and their unresolved constituent/Maxtree rights remain private and unchanged; the new CC0 sources do not clear those historical rights. No Foggy library asset is newly placed by this recipe.

Private originals and working files: `Backups/ExteriorComposition_20261110/source/`, `exports/`, `exterior-adapted.blend`. Original range download: `https://files.innerscene.com/library/mountain-range-ac633cbe/07a2fa190daad03e0966/mountain-range.glb`, 11,380,492 bytes, SHA256 `9b77ffa30a0878b5d7e8f33228451ba39658534947fc1646643e445ba2279fd2`. `download-manifest.json` records actual Poly Haven downloads, sizes and SHA256; their supplied MD5 checks passed. Original source files were not edited or added to Git. Candidate BlenderKit downloads were unavailable without account authorization and were not incorporated.
